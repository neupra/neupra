## 👩‍🔬 About Me

I'm a Data Science undergraduate at **United International University (UIU)** interested in a central question:

> **How do humans and AI reason under uncertainty—and how can we design AI that people can use, question, and trust appropriately?**

My interests sit at the intersection of **Human–AI Interaction, Bayesian and cognitive modelling, trustworthy AI, and responsible machine learning**. I enjoy exploring not only whether a model performs well, but also how people interpret its outputs, when they rely on it, and what responsible evaluation should look like.

## 🔬 Research

<details open>
<summary><b>01 · TrustCal — Trust-Calibrated AI for Medical Learning</b> <i>(Summer 2026–Present)</i></summary>

**Role:** Undergraduate Researcher · HCI & Human–AI Interaction

- Investigating trust dynamics, cognitive offloading, verification behaviours, and clinical reasoning in human–AI interaction.
- **Mixed-methods study:** survey (target *N* ≈ 150) and interviews (target *N* ≈ 20), ongoing.
- Designing **TrustCal**, a learning interface exploring Socratic reasoning modes and uncertainty indicators.
- Controlled user study (planned *N* ≈ 60); manuscript in preparation.

**Research question:** How might an AI learning interface help learners reason more deeply instead of relying on answers uncritically?

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>02 · Green AI & Cognitive Alignment — Banglish Hate Speech Reasoning</b> <i>(Summer 2026–Present)</i></summary>

**Role:** Undergraduate Researcher · Computational Linguistics & Green Computing  
**Supervisor:** Md. Tarek Hasan

- Comparing human reasoning structures with outputs from large and small language models.
- Exploring demographic factors such as age, education, and screen time.
- Evaluating performance alongside energy consumption, carbon footprint, electricity and water usage, and GPU/CPU efficiency.
- Manuscript in preparation.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>03 · Mind vs. Model — Human Reasoning vs. LLMs on Banglish Hate Speech</b> <i>(Spring 2025)</i></summary>

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · LLM Evaluation

- Cleaned real-world Banglish hate-speech data: 22 raw columns reduced to 7 usable features; handled 1,800+ missing values.
- Built a human-annotation pipeline using Google Forms, Apps Script, and GitHub Pages.
- Compared GPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek against human annotations.
- In the reported evaluation, GPT-4 achieved **78.49% accuracy** and **75.50% Macro-F1**. Limited human–LLM agreement highlighted the challenge of interpreting informal, code-mixed language.

</details>
